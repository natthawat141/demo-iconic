# Product

## Register

product

## Users

ICONIC team members ask recurring sales and client-care questions while working in LINE-first, interruption-heavy daily routines. Team leaders and knowledge managers turn the founder's working knowledge into approved, reusable guidance. AION3 presenters use the same interface in a client meeting to demonstrate the complete knowledge loop and discover production requirements.

## Product Purpose

Nong Fah is an internal Thai-language knowledge assistant prototype. It demonstrates that ICONIC can capture knowledge, approve it, answer team questions only from approved material, reveal the supporting source, refuse unsupported questions, and convert unanswered questions into new approved knowledge.

Success is a client understanding and trying the full loop without technical explanation: ask, answer with evidence, escalate a gap, add and approve knowledge, then ask again and receive the newly grounded answer.

## Brand Personality

Grounded, calm, expert. The product should feel like a trusted team lead who communicates clearly and knows when to stop, not an all-knowing AI persona. The meeting experience should inspire confidence without pretending the prototype is production-ready.

## Anti-references

- A generic ChatGPT clone with no visible ICONIC workflow or evidence.
- Neon, futuristic, glass-heavy AI dashboards that make the model the product.
- Dense enterprise CRM screens that bury the primary task in configuration.
- Cute chatbot styling, emoji controls, or playful copy that weakens trust.
- Fabricated confidence scores or UI that disguises demo fixtures as live production data.

## Design Principles

1. **Evidence before eloquence:** sources and knowledge status are first-class, not footnotes.
2. **Make uncertainty useful:** an unsupported answer becomes a clear next action and a knowledge gap.
3. **Keep the loop visible:** users should always understand how questions become governed knowledge.
4. **Familiar work-tool behavior:** navigation, forms, tables, and status language should require no learning ceremony.
5. **Prototype honestly:** polished enough to earn trust, explicit enough that nobody mistakes it for production.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for the implemented surfaces. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic labels, adequate contrast, screen-reader status announcements, reduced motion, and touch targets of at least 44px. Thai text must remain readable at normal browser zoom and layouts must not rely on color alone to communicate status.
